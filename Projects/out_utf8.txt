Title-
 
Parking Finder & Booking App
Abstract
Finding a parking space in busy cities is often difficult, time-consuming, and frustrating. Drivers may spend a significant amount of time searching for available parking, especially near shopping malls, offices, hospitals, colleges, and other crowded locations. This can lead to traffic congestion, fuel wastage, unnecessary delays, and stress. At the same time, many parking spaces remain underutilized because drivers do not have an easy way to know about their availability.
The 
Parking Finder & Booking App
 is proposed as a smart solution that helps users find and reserve available parking spaces through a simple digital platform. The application allows users to search for parking spaces based on their current location or selected destination. It displays important information such as parking location, availability, distance, pricing, and operating hours. Users can select a suitable parking slot, book it for a specific duration, and receive a booking confirmation.
The system can also provide parking-space management features for parking owners or administrators, allowing them to add parking locations, update slot availability, manage bookings, and monitor occupancy. The application can include secure user authentication and booking history.
Overall, the proposed system aims to make parking 
faster, easier, and more organized
, while helping parking providers manage their available spaces efficiently and reducing unnecessary traffic and fuel consumption.
